USING AN IPHONE AS THE CAMERA (WINDOWS)
=======================================

Windows has no Continuity Camera, so we use Camo to make the iPhone show
up as a normal webcam that Live Studio can open.

1. INSTALL CAMO
   - iPhone: install "Camo" from the App Store.
   - PC: install "Camo Studio" from https://reincubate.com/camo/
   - Connect the iPhone to the PC with a USB cable (more reliable than Wi-Fi).
   - Open Camo on the iPhone and Camo Studio on the PC. Camo Studio should
     show the iPhone's live video. Select the iPhone as the source if needed.

2. KEEP CAMO RUNNING
   - Leave the Camo app open in the foreground on the iPhone, screen unlocked.
     Camo pauses when the app is backgrounded or the phone locks.
   - Close other apps that might be using Camo (Zoom, Discord, browser tabs).
   - A crossed-out-eye image means Camo is paused.

3. SELECT IT IN LIVE STUDIO
   - Start the app from Interface_updated:
         winvenv310\Scripts\python.exe studio_main.py
   - Click "Calibrate Cone" (top-right of the home page).
   - Set "Index" to the Camo camera and click "Start Camera".
       * Camo is usually index 1; the laptop webcam is usually index 0.
         The numbers depend on which cameras are connected, so if you see the
         wrong camera, click "Stop Camera", change the index, and try again.
   - If the preview stays black or shows an error, change "Backend" to
     "DSHOW (DirectShow)" and start the camera again.

4. TIPS FOR CALIBRATION
   - Hold or mount the iPhone in LANDSCAPE. Portrait wastes most of the image
     in black side bars.
   - Mount it at the audience's eye height and distance, facing the cone,
     and do not move it during or after calibration.
   - Turn off Camo effects (background blur, auto-framing, filters). Lock
     exposure, focus and white balance in Camo Studio if available.
   - Keep the Camo logo (bottom-right corner) outside the yellow box.
   - Our test feed was 1280x720, which is enough to calibrate.


NO CABLE? RECORD NOW, CALIBRATE LATER
=====================================

If the phone is too far from the PC for USB (and Camo over Wi-Fi is blocked,
as on eduroam), record the patterns with the phone's normal Camera app and
load the video afterwards. Camo is not needed for this.

1. SET UP THE PHONE
   - Tripod at the audience's eye height and distance, LANDSCAPE, facing the
     cone. Don't touch it again until the recording is done.
   - Camera app > Video, 1080p at 30 fps is plenty (Settings > Camera >
     Record Video).
   - Press and hold on the cone reflection until "AE/AF LOCK" appears, so
     exposure and focus don't change when the TV goes black.
   - Dim the room lights.

2. PLAY AND RECORD
   - In Live Studio, open the Calibrate tab and pick the TV under "Display".
   - Under "Record now, calibrate later", set "Black lead-in" long enough to
     walk to the phone (default 10 s).
   - Click "Play for Recording". The TV goes black: start recording now.
     The green, magenta and striped patterns play next (about 40 s).
   - Keep recording until the TV goes black again, then stop.

3. LOAD AND BUILD
   - Copy the video to the PC (AirDrop isn't on Windows: use iCloud Photos,
     a USB cable afterwards, or Google Drive/OneDrive).
   - Click "Load Recording..." with the SAME display still selected. The app
     finds the patterns by itself and shows the solid green frame.
   - Drag the yellow box around the cone reflection only, then "Build Map".
   - Use "Grid with correction" and look at the cone from the phone's spot
     to check it, then "Save profile".
   - If the video won't open, set Settings > Camera > Formats > Most
     Compatible on the iPhone and record again.
